The Story of Sunday Ojima Onalo

Known creatively as Sunny Ojima Onalo

"Life is shaped by an enduring belief in the beauty and possibilities of life."

Sunday is a Nigerian businessman, poet, motivational speaker and experienced marketing professional whose life has been shaped by an enduring belief in the beauty and possibilities of life.

With more than two decades in marketing and sales, he has built a career around understanding people, creating relationships and helping businesses and individuals see opportunities where others may see limitations.

He is an experienced insurance executive and an IPMAN Fellow, and is pursuing a Doctorate in Commerce. Beyond professional accomplishments, he considers his greatest responsibilities to be human ones: family, mentorship, generosity and helping people find their footing in life.

"Achievement without purpose is incomplete, and success carries a responsibility to others."

His relationship with God influences how he approaches life, family, work, relationships and service.

He believes achievement without purpose is incomplete and that success carries a responsibility to others. His faith keeps him grateful, hopeful and committed to doing good.

"Relationships, responsibility, love and togetherness are among life's greatest treasures."

Family is at the centre of his life, both nuclear and extended.

He considers family relationships and mutual responsibility to be the anchor of genuine peace and shared joy.

"There is always something to learn, and there is always something to share."

Those who know him describe an extraordinary appetite for knowledge across business, marketing, insurance, sports, poetry, people, society, history, personal development and everyday life.

"Every individual has a story, values and experiences that can be developed into a meaningful identity."

His own journey blends business, communication, relationships, service, faith and personal development.
