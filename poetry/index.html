Poems by Sunny Ojima Onalo

Words from a life fully lived.

Poems by Sunny Ojima Onalo is a collection shaped by reflection, experience, emotion, faith, humanity and the world around him.

The poems explore joy and sorrow, people and places, loss and hope, society and personal experience. They invite the reader to pause. To think. To feel. And sometimes, simply to remember the beauty of being alive.

Why This Book?

For Sunny, poetry is not merely about beautiful words. It is about seeing.

Seeing people.

Seeing pain.

Seeing beauty.

Seeing possibility.

Seeing God.

Featured Excerpt

When morning breaks upon the quiet road,

And footsteps carry more than just the load,

We find the strength not in the things we hold,

But in the faith that turns the gray to gold.


No storm outlives the promise of the sun,

No race is lost until the fight is done.

— From Poems by Sunny Ojima Onalo
